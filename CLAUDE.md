# Who I'm working with — read this first, every session

This repository is the personal-brand and content workspace of a **Doctor turned Psychosomatic & Performance-Based Coach and Speaker, based in the UAE**. Don't ask them to re-explain themselves — everything below is already decided. Build on it.

## Positioning (fixed)
- **Niche:** Psychosomatic & Performance-Based Coaching and Speaking.
- **Integrated model (the differentiator):** Psychosomatics + Nervous System Regulation + Human Performance. Everyone else talks about these separately; they bring them together.
- **Core belief:** Performance is not just discipline, motivation or mindset. A person is an integrated mind-body system.
- **Central brand message:** *"Understanding yourself is the foundation of improving yourself."*
- **Credibility:** Medical Doctor + NLP certifications (American Board) + CPD (UK). Uses NLP and Timeline Therapy where appropriate.
- **Desired association:** Healthcare credibility + Psychosomatic expertise + Performance coaching + Entrepreneurship + Technology.

## Never position them as
- A generic mindset / motivational coach or an influencer who talks about everything.
- Someone making exaggerated health claims. Stress *influences* health; it is not the sole cause of disease, and regulation does not guarantee prevention.
- A therapist. Coaching ≠ therapy or medical treatment; panic disorder, severe anxiety and clinical conditions get referred to licensed professionals.
- Someone whose AI/business talk drowns out the coaching identity.

## ICP
Financially established, ambitious, solution-aware individuals with urgency — **especially women**: entrepreneurs, founders, CEOs, executives, high-income professionals, financially independent women in the UAE. Looks successful outside, feels anxious, overwhelmed, dysregulated or stuck inside — or is already doing well and wants to operate at a higher level.
Formula: **Pain + Purchasing Power + Solution Awareness + Urgency.** Not targeting budget/mass-market or students without spending power.
They value privacy, scientific credibility, practical tools (not hype), premium experience, measurable progress.
