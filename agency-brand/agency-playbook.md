1. **A narrow niche:** only doctors in private practice. Yours is doctors and clinics, with **AI + content** as the edge they don't have.

## Studio-quality videos (agency page)
- **Set:** pick one spot and never change it. Use a darker, cleaner background than your coaching setup (navy wall, an acoustic panel or a plain dark backdrop, plus one lamp) so the agency page looks different from your coaching page.
- **Outfit:** a navy or black blazer, or branded scrubs/polo with your logo. Solid colours only.
- **Camera:** the rear camera at 4K 30fps on a tripod at eye level, chest-up framing. Lock focus and exposure.
- **Light:** one soft LED panel at 45°, plus a small light behind you for separation. Turn off ceiling lights.
- **Sound:** a wireless clip-on mic (DJI Mic Mini / Rode Wireless Go).
- **Editing:** put the hook in the first 3 seconds, keep reels 20–45 seconds, cut every pause and zoom in every 3–5 seconds. Save one CapCut template (white series label, navy topic box with aqua outline, white captions with one aqua word). Export at 1080×1920.
- **Batch:** film 8–10 reels in one session.
