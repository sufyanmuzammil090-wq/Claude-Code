# Brand Playbook: building a recognisable page (modelled on @thecontentdoctors)

## Why their page works
1. **One look, every time.** Same studio, same uniform, same framing and same light. You know it's them before you read anything.
2. **Named series.** "Red flag or green flag", "S to F tier", "Myth or fact", "True or false", "Rated 1 to 10", "Worth it or not". Each series is a ready-made hook, and you never run out of ideas.
3. **The same text system on every reel.** A small white label at the top names the series, a dark rounded box holds the topic, and big captions run underneath.
4. **Three post types in rotation.** Talking-head reels, tweet-style quote posts and designed carousels.
5. **Three pinned posts with cinematic, bold type.** They tell a new visitor what the page is about.
6. **A narrow niche.** Content for doctors in private practice, and nothing else.

## Brand colours
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Midnight Teal | `#0E3B43` | Topic boxes, carousel backgrounds, headings |
| Base | Warm Sand | `#F2E8DA` | Backgrounds, series labels. Matches your wall. |
| Accent | Soft Gold | `#C9A15B` | One highlighted word per caption, handles, small details |

Use near-black `#141C1E` and white as neutrals. Gold is only an accent and should never be used for long text. Never put gold text on sand, because it's hard to read.

**Fonts:** Inter Black or Montserrat ExtraBold for reel text. Inter SemiBold for quote posts. Optionally, a serif such as Playfair Display for pinned-post headlines.

## Making studio-quality videos with a phone
**Setup (build it once and never move it)**
- **Background:** your sand textured wall is already on-brand. Use the same spot every time, and add one plant or lamp for depth.
- **Outfit:** solid colours (teal, sand, black, cream) with your hijab. Busy prints pull attention from your face and break up when Instagram compresses the video.
- **Camera:** the rear camera at 4K 30fps on a tripod at eye level, 1–1.5 m away, framed from the chest up. Leave space above your head for text. Long-press the screen to lock focus and exposure. Wipe the lens first.
- **Light:** face a window, or use one soft LED panel at 45°, slightly above eye level. Turn off the yellow ceiling lights.
- **Sound:** a wireless lapel mic (DJI Mic Mini, Rode Wireless Go or similar). Bad sound loses viewers faster than a soft image.

**Filming**
- Put the hook in the first 3 seconds, both spoken and as on-screen text.
- Keep reels 20–45 seconds and cut every pause.
- Zoom in slightly every 3–5 seconds.
- Batch-film 8–10 reels in a 2-hour session with one or two outfit changes.

**Editing (CapCut)**
- Save one template with the series label (sand box, teal text), the topic box (teal, gold outline) and captions (white, black outline, one gold word).
- Export at 1080×1920, 30fps, high bitrate. In Instagram settings, turn on "Upload at highest quality".
