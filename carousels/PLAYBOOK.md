# Dr. Azka — Carousel Playbook

Built 3 Nov 2026 from 8 reference carousels (5 × @dariamedric, 3 × @thecontentdoctors)
plus Dr. Azka's own brief, November plan and positioning files.
Use it for the 3 daily carousels. Learn the patterns, never copy their topics or wording.

## 0. The goal every carousel serves
**3–5 paying clinic clients (pilot or low-ticket) from the UAE + USA by 31 Dec 2026.**
Every carousel must move a clinic owner one step: notice → trust → DM → audit → pilot call → client.

Conversion path:
- Carousel → comment (question CTA) or DM keyword **AUDIT**
- AUDIT = free Patient-Leak Audit: Azka tests the clinic like a patient (9 PM DM, price question, missed call) and sends a short report.
- Audit report → 15-min call → 60-day pilot.

Planning targets (not promises): ~40 qualified DM conversations → ~15 audits/calls → 3–5 pilots.

Weekly mix (21 carousels): 7 Content (reach), 7 Branding/Authority (trust), 7 Leak/AI (conversion, AUDIT CTA).
At least 2/week are proof posts built from REAL audit findings (never invented).

## 0c. Daria Medric deep-dive (added 20 Nov, from Azka's analysis)
- **One central belief, repeated relentlessly.** Hers: expertise alone isn't enough, doctors must become recognisable.
  Ours: *Content gets you the opportunity. Your systems decide what happens to it.*
- **Sell the future, not the service:** "the doctor patients remember, trust and want to book".
- **Hooks are diagnoses, not topics:** name a problem they feel but can't explain.
- **Post formula:** Pain → Reframe → New belief → Small promise → CTA.
- **One repeated conversion mechanism** (hers: comment/DM "KNOWN" for a challenge). We need ONE named offer + keyword used on every post.
- **Three narratives:** (1) the doctor's brand, (2) the clinic brand = the whole patient experience, (3) the patient pathway (reply, booking, follow-up, reactivation). Never sell "AI"; sell the fix to the leak.
- **Every script must answer at least one:** what's costing patients · what makes the doctor interchangeable · what confuses the patient · what damages trust · what slows booking · what happens after the enquiry · what isn't followed up · what could be easier (no medical advice).

- 10-slot daily rhythm (if volume increases): patient loss · doctor pain · specialty insight · clinic-brand lesson · patient trust · content lesson · reception/follow-up · automation outcome · contrarian belief · diagnostic question.

## 4. Slide rules (merged)
- 5–6 slides, 4:5 (1080×1350). Purple style: DR. AZKA top-left, counter top-right, arrow bottom-right.
- **Slide 1:** max ~15 words. Contrarian / number / scene / "X vs Y". Optional one-line bar under it.
- **Value slides:** headline (numbered `01 —`) + ONE structure device + **punch line**.
- **Every line must teach, prove or give a step.** If a line does none, cut it.
- **Last slide:** final point + CTA box.
- Numbers: real, sourced, or labelled "*Illustrative example*".
- UAE/US rules mentioned → "Educational, not legal advice" + remind Azka to verify.
- No guarantees, no real patient data, never "ask only happy patients for reviews".

## 5. CTA options
- **Never** "tell me below" / "comment below" — doctors won't type. Use: a one-tap question + a complimentary expert offer,
  e.g. "DM me, and I'll turn them into your 7-day content plan."
- **Default:** one-word / one-number question ("Which leak is biggest in your clinic? 1–5").
- **Keyword lead magnet (Daria-style):** only once a real resource exists (e.g. the after-hours reply template, the patient-leak checklist). Use one keyword consistently.

## 6. Daily workflow (locked)
1. Claude sends 3 scripts: Content + Clinic Branding + Patient Leak/AI.
2. Dr. Azka and Claude improve them line by line.
3. On approval, Claude designs them in the purple style (1080×1350).
4. Claude sends every slide as a separate picture, in posting order.
5. Files saved in `carousels/YYYY-MM-DD-cN/` and pushed to the repo.

Quality bar for every carousel:
- Slide 1 stops a clinic owner: number, scene, contrarian or "X vs Y".
- Each value slide = headline + one device + punch line.
- At least one copy-paste template (reply, hook, script line) per carousel.
- One clear idea per carousel, tied back to the patient journey.
- Final check: would a dermatologist screenshot this and send it to her team?
