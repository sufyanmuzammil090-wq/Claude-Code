# Dr. Azka — Carousel Playbook

Built 3 Nov 2026 from 8 reference carousels (5 × @dariamedric, 3 × @thecontentdoctors)
plus Dr. Azka's own brief, November plan and positioning files.
Use it for the 3 daily carousels. Learn the patterns, never copy their topics or wording.

## 1. What the references do well

| Pattern | Where seen | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Slide 1 = one bold sentence, no visual** | Daria (all 5) | Stops the scroll. Contrarian, list promise, "how to", or "X vs Y". |
| **Every slide ends with a punch line** | Daria | Bigger, highlighted closing line. Each slide is screenshot-able on its own. |
| **A structure device on every value slide** | Both | Two columns, ✗/✓ list, "Instead of → Try" table, numbered rows, illustrative bar chart, equation. |
| **Rewrites (✗ old line → ✓ better line)** | Content Doctors (9 slides) | A template doctors can copy today: high saves/shares. |
| **Swipe files (lists of ideas)** | Content Doctors "70 reel ideas", Daria "10 ideas" | Highest shares (148–224). Doctors send them to their team. |
| **"Why it works" line under examples** | Content Doctors | Teaches the principle, not only the tip. |
| **Illustrative numbers are labelled** | Daria ("*Illustrative, not market data") | Keeps credibility. Matches our no-unsourced-stats rule. |
| **Recap slide: "Where should you start?"** | Content Doctors | Turns the lesson into one action. |
| **Same CTA every post (keyword lead magnet)** | Daria ("KNOWN") | Builds a list. Needs a real offer behind it. |

## 2. Their gap = Dr. Azka's edge
Both accounts stop at **content and positioning**. Nobody talks about what happens **after** the patient sees the content:
reply speed, price DMs, follow-up, no-shows, reactivation, clinic branding across 4–5 doctors.
That's the merge: **their format + Azka's full patient journey.**
> Content gets you the opportunity. Your systems decide what happens to it.

## 3. Daily mix (3 carousels)
1. **Content** — hooks, rewrites, swipe files, what to post (share-driver, TOFU)
2. **Clinic branding / positioning** — doctor-led clinic, lanes, trust, premium (TOFU/MOFU)
3. **Patient leak / AI systems** — reply, follow-up, no-shows, reactivation, numbers (MOFU/BOFU)

Rotate the 10 pillars inside these 3 slots. Once a week, slot 2 can be Dr. Azka's story.

## 4. Slide rules (merged)
- 5–6 slides, 4:5 (1080×1350). Purple style: DR. AZKA top-left, counter top-right, arrow bottom-right.
- **Slide 1:** max ~15 words. Contrarian / number / scene / "X vs Y". Optional one-line bar under it.
- **Value slides:** headline (numbered `01 —`) + ONE structure device + **punch line**.
- **Every line must teach, prove or give a step.** If a line does none, cut it.
- **Last slide:** final point + CTA box.
- Numbers: real, sourced, or labelled "*Illustrative example*".
- UAE/US rules mentioned → "Educational, not legal advice" + remind Azka to verify.
- No guarantees, no real patient data, never "ask only happy patients for reviews".

## 5. CTA options
- **Default:** one-word / one-number question ("Which leak is biggest in your clinic? 1–5").
- **Keyword lead magnet (Daria-style):** only once a real resource exists (e.g. the after-hours reply template, the patient-leak checklist). Use one keyword consistently.

## 6. Daily workflow (locked)
1. Claude sends 3 scripts: Content + Clinic Branding + Patient Leak/AI.
2. Dr. Azka and Claude improve them line by line.
3. On approval, Claude designs them in the purple style (1080×1350).
4. Claude sends every slide as a separate picture, in posting order.
5. Files saved in `carousels/YYYY-MM-DD-cN/` and pushed to the repo.

Quality bar for every carousel:
- Slide 1 stops a clinic owner: number, scene, contrarian or "X vs Y".
- Each value slide = headline + one device + punch line.
- At least one copy-paste template (reply, hook, script line) per carousel.
- One clear idea per carousel, tied back to the patient journey.
- Final check: would a dermatologist screenshot this and send it to her team?
