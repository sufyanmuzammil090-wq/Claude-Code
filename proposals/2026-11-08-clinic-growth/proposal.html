The problem we solve

Leads in, patients out

Clinics spend on content and ads, then lose patients after the enquiry arrives. Patients compare replies, not credentials. The fastest helpful clinic usually wins.

Most patients research in the evening, after the front desk has gone home. A missed call, a next-day reply or a bare price is often enough to send them to the next clinic.

Most agencies stop at content. We cover the full patient journey: the post that gets noticed, the reply at 9 PM, the follow-up and the rebooking months later.

The automated systems we built

Five systems.
Five leaks closed.

One system for each point where clinics most often lose patients. Each runs on its own, and the clinic team takes over when a person is needed.

✗ Leak 01
A missed call. No text back.
✓ Missed-call text-back
A friendly text within 60 seconds.

Calls missed during busy hours or after closing get an automatic reply asking how the clinic can help, so the patient doesn't call the next clinic.

✗ Leak 02
A 9 PM DM. A next-day reply.
✓ After-hours instant reply
An instant first reply, day or night.

Enquiries after closing are answered straight away. The team picks the conversation up in the morning.

✗ Leak 03
"How much?" Just a price back.
✓ Booking-focused price reply
A price range + one question + two times.

Price-only replies get compared. Helpful replies that offer appointment times get booked.

✗ Leak 04
"I'll think about it." Silence.
✓ Follow-up sequence
Polite follow-ups, then stop.

✗ Leak 05
Treated once. Never contacted again.
✓ Rebooking reminder
A reminder timed to the treatment.

Past patients are invited back at the right moment. The easiest new patient is one the clinic already treated.

These systems support the front desk; they don't replace it. Every message is written in the clinic's own voice and approved before it goes live.
