## 2. The problem: clinics are paying for attention and losing the patient after it

Private healthcare in the UAE and the Gulf is growing and competitive. Clinics invest in content, ads and influencers to be seen. But a patient's decision doesn't end at the post. It continues through a journey most clinics never look at:

1. **Discovery.** The patient sees a doctor or clinic online.
2. **Trust.** They check the doctor's profile and ask themselves "Is this person credible? Are they right for me?"
3. **First contact.** A WhatsApp or Instagram DM, often after hours. "How much is it?" "Does it hurt?"
4. **Reply.** Slow, generic or price-only replies lose the patient to the next clinic.
5. **Booking and attendance.** No reminders, no confirmations, so there are no-shows.
6. **Follow-up and return.** No reactivation, so a patient who came once never returns.

I call these **patient leaks**. ## 4. What the agency does

### Pillar A: Doctor & clinic branding
There are **two types of branding in healthcare**, and a clinic owner or private-practice doctor needs both:
1. **Personal branding for the doctor**: positioning, content and authority that make patients trust a specific doctor.
2. **Clinic branding built around its specialists**: a clinic that patients choose because of the doctors inside it.

This includes positioning, content strategy and scripts, and compliant, medically accurate messaging.

### Pillar B: AI patient-journey systems
Automated, agentic AI systems that close the leaks:
- Instant, on-brand replies to WhatsApp, Instagram and web enquiries, 24/7, including after hours
- Answers to price and "doubt" questions (pain, results, safety), using content the doctor has already filmed
- Booking, confirmations and reminders that reduce no-shows
- Follow-up and reactivation of past patients
- Dashboards showing enquiries, response times, bookings and return rate

The AI supports the clinic team and the doctor. It doesn't replace clinical judgement. It never gives medical advice and hands off to humans where it should.

### Pillar C: Patient-Leak Audit (entry point)
A free or low-cost audit. I test the clinic as a patient would (a 9 PM DM, a price question, a missed call) and deliver a short report showing where patients are lost. This leads to a **60-day pilot**, then an ongoing engagement.
