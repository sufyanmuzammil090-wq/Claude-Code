04  What we doFounder Thesis · Dr. Azka
The model

One medical partner
for the entire patient journey.

Brand

Building clinics around specialists

There are two types of branding in healthcare, and every clinic owner or private-practice doctor needs both:

  • Clinic branding built around its specialists, so the doctors become the reason patients choose the clinic.
  • Personal branding for each doctor: positioning, content strategy and scripts.

Written by medical professionals, so it's medically accurate and aligned with UAE health-advertising rules.

Systems

Automated patient journeys

Built in-house by our software and AI engineers:

  • Instant, on-brand replies on WhatsApp, Instagram and web, 24/7
  • Answers to price and "doubt" questions, using the doctor's own short videos
  • Booking, confirmations and reminders that reduce no-shows
  • Follow-up and reactivation of past patients
  • A clear dashboard: enquiries, reply time, bookings, return rate

How a clinic starts: Patient-Leak Audit → 60-day pilot

We test the clinic exactly as a patient would: a 9 PM message, a price question, a missed call. Then we deliver a short report showing where patients are lost. That leads to a 60-day pilot with agreed metrics (reply time, booking rate, no-shows, reactivation) and then an ongoing partnership.

Safety by design

Our systems handle enquiries, logistics and doctor-approved information only. They never diagnose or give medical advice, and anything clinical goes straight to the clinic's licensed staff. Patient data is handled in line with UAE data-protection and DHA requirements.

05  Filling Gap 03Founder Thesis · Dr. Azka
The growth opportunity · Gap 03 · Medical tourism

The International Patient Desk

FCI's analysis identifies medical tourism as an open gap: international patients need help with specialist discovery, appointment coordination, travel planning and aftercare. It's the same front door we already fix for local patients, with higher stakes and more steps. That makes it the natural next step for our agency.

What the desk does for Dubai clinics

1
EnquiryAnswered in the patient's language and time zone
2
Medical reviewOur medical team reviews reports and needs
3
Specialist matchThe right specialist and verified clinic
4
CoordinationAppointments, travel timing, pre-visit prep
5
AftercareStructured follow-up once the patient is home

Why we can win it

  • Medical professionals at the front. International patients send reports and clinical questions before they travel. A medical team can read them and route correctly; a travel or marketing agency can't.
  • Non-clinical by design. We coordinate and the licensed clinic treats, so patient safety stays with the clinician.
  • Built on what we already run. Same systems, same team, same specialist-led clinic brands.

How we'll start (FCI's playbook)

  • One source market, e.g. [Saudi Arabia / Qatar / home country]
  • One treatment category within our starting specialties
  • Licensed clinic partners in Dubai, with the right facilitator licensing confirmed with DHA
  • Measured outcomes: enquiry-to-booking rate, time to appointment, patient satisfaction, aftercare completion

Where investment would go

The core agency grows on client revenue. The International Patient Desk needs a dedicated product, and this is where investment would accelerate us:

  • Additional developers to build a multilingual patient portal with secure report upload
  • A specialist-matching and coordination engine connected to partner clinics
  • A pilot in one source market, with dedicated medical coordinators
